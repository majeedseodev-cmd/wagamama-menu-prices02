Author: Eleanor Finch

UK Food Menu Analyst & Senior Editor

Eleanor Finch is a UK food menu analyst and editorial specialist focused on helping everyday diners make smarter decisions in casual dining environments.

Professional Focus

Her work centres on three areas: practical order planning, nutrition-aware reading of menus, and realistic value guidance for households and individuals. She writes with a consumer-first approach that avoids hype and prioritises usable clarity.

Editorial Principles

Eleanor follows an evidence-minded editorial method that emphasises transparent assumptions, plain language, and practical outcomes. Every guide is built so readers can use it during real meal decisions, not only as general background reading.
